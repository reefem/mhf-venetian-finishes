Bespoke, by hand

No rolls. No repeating patterns. Just one-of-a-kind walls.

At MHF Venetian Finishes, every wall is created by hand. Layer by layer, we build up depth, movement and natural veining, with a final finish chosen to suit your design. Every job is different, and so is every wall.

Paint is perfect when a wall is meant to sit quietly in the background. When you want one surface to become a real design feature, Venetian plaster gives you a richness that flat colour simply can't recreate.

From a single fireplace to a full restaurant fit-out, we take care with every detail, keep pricing clear and upfront, and are happy to give you a free, no-obligation quote.
